The hands behind the work

There is beauty
in what remains.

D'Yeslo began with a question: what if the things we throw away could hold the most meaning?

From a small studio in the Hudson Valley, artist Yesenia Lo shapes reclaimed bottles and found materials into objects that make a room feel more lived-in, more considered, more yours.
